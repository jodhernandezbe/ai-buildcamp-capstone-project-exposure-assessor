# UI audit, 2026-10-30

First run of `/ui-audit` on the EcoFate frontend. Reference: `frontend/DESIGN.md`; heuristics
H1 to H10 from the `ui-craft` skill (`references/audit-heuristics.md`). The audit changed no
application code.

## Scope and method

- **Live app**: built frontend served by `uv run python -m api` at the configured loopback
  address, driven by Playwright (the frontend's own dev dependency) in light and dark at
  1440 × 900 and 720 × 900. No API key, so the app is in its first-load state: shell, overview
  (empty), map (empty), chat welcome and composer, settings drawer, monitoring (Logfire sessions
  present), fullscreen of overview and map, copilot hidden, keyboard focus after three tabs.
  Script: `openspec/changes/add-ui-craft-workflow/audit-render.mjs`; screenshots were written to
  the session scratch directory (18 files, `<surface>-<state>-<scheme>.png`).
- **Approved references** from `test:visual`: overview ribbon (empty and ready, 4 images) and
  exposure chain (5 states × 2 schemes), looked at for the ready states.
- **Static checks** across `frontend/src`: literal colours, shadows, easings and font families
  (none outside `BrandLogo`); `?? 0` fallbacks (counts and offsets only, plus one age-band share);
  controls without names (none of 25 visible); live regions (one at load); landmarks (nav, panel
  headers, named regions for every card and the map).
- **Not reached**: anything that needs an assessment or a key (see "Not assessed").

Scores: 2 good, 1 partial, 0 poor, `–` not testable in this run. Totals are out of the scored
heuristics.

## Ranked proposals

1. **Overview first load shows an empty band and hides the five summary cards** (Overview
   ribbon, H1 H2 H10; also fullscreen and narrow).
   - Observation: the grid's rows are `minmax(max-content, 1fr)` and the worst-case section spans
     the first row, so with no scenarios one sentence takes the whole visible panel
     (`app-first-load-*`, `overview-fullscreen-*`, `app-narrow-*`); the risk, worker, chemical,
     population and meteorology cards start below the fold. In the ready references the same
     row leaves a band about a third of the panel high (`ribbon-ready-*`).
   - Proposal: let an empty section take its content height (`auto` row, or render the
     worst-case section as a one-line `Banner`-style note when there are no scenarios) and keep
     `1fr` only for rows with content.
   - Design document: add to Materials: "an empty section takes its content height; rows grow
     only when they hold data".
   - Effort: S. Value: high (first impression of every session, three layouts).

2. **The map's "USA" control shows through the Monitoring view** (Monitoring, H3 H9).
   - Observation: `monitoring-light.png` shows the map's basemap chip over the sessions table at
     mid-right. `SplitWorkspace` covers the analysis area with `z-10`
     (`components/layout/SplitWorkspace.tsx:100`) while the map's chip is `z-20`
     (`features/map/ExposureMapCanvas.tsx:226`) and the legend `z-10`.
   - Proposal: give the covering layer a z-index above every map overlay, or make the map
     region its own stacking context (`isolation: isolate`) so its inner z-indexes cannot escape.
   - Design document: add a layering ladder to Materials (panel content < map overlays < covering
     view < drawers and dialogs).
   - Effort: S. Value: high (visible defect on every Monitoring visit).

3. **Monitoring table overflows the pane and repeats a header** (Monitoring, H2 H7 H9).
   - Observation: at 1440 px the table is wider than the analysis area; the columns after
     "Turns" are cut and two headers read "MODEL" (`monitoring-light.png`;
     `features/monitoring/SessionsTable.tsx:30-35`, headers are `whitespace-nowrap`).
   - Proposal: fit the table to its container (hide secondary columns under a container query
     and keep them in the row's detail, or horizontal scroll with a sticky first column); give
     every header a distinct short name.
   - Design document: none.
   - Effort: M. Value: medium (one view, used often during development).

4. **Settings drawer opens almost empty** (Settings, H1 H2 H4 H10).
   - Observation: `settings-drawer-*` show a full-height drawer with two collapsed rows,
     "Assistant" and "Data services"; nothing says whether a key is saved.
   - Proposal: open the first section by default, or show a one-line status on each collapsed
     row ("OpenAI key saved", "Census key missing") using `StatusPill` words; size the drawer to
     its content until a section is open.
   - Design document: none.
   - Effort: S. Value: medium (first task of a new user is the key).

5. **Map legend shows four zero counts before anything was assessed** (Map, H4).
   - Observation: `app-first-load-*` and `map-fullscreen-*`: "Below concern 0, Near concern 0,
     Above concern 0, Not computed 0". The counts are real (no pins), but they read as data in a
     UI whose rule is "never a zero that could be read as a value".
   - Proposal: when there are no facilities, replace the counts with one line ("No facilities
     mapped yet") and show counts only once pins exist.
   - Design document: Copy section: "counts of items are shown only when items exist".
   - Effort: S. Value: medium.

6. **Disabled rail actions do not say why** (Shell, H4 H10).
   - Observation: "Clear workspace" and "Generate report" are dimmed at first load with no hint
     (`app-first-load-*`).
   - Proposal: add an `aria-description` and a tooltip through `InfoTooltip` wording: "Nothing
     to clear yet", "Run an assessment first".
   - Design document: Required states, Interactive row: "disabled controls say why".
   - Effort: S. Value: low to medium.

7. **Pathway chips in the chat header use tracked uppercase and middle dots** (Chat, H3 H7).
   - Observation: "AIR • WATER • SOIL" under the EcoFate title (`app-first-load-*`), against the
     document's anti-patterns (uppercase outside titles, decorative separators).
   - Proposal: sentence-case pills: "Air", "Water (planned)", "Soil (planned)", the planned ones
     in `text-fg2`.
   - Design document: none (already covered).
   - Effort: S. Value: low.

8. **Exposure chain edge labels are below the smallest documented size** (Exposure chain, H2).
   - Observation: the value pills on edges ("Peak concentration 3 µg/m³") render at roughly
     9 px in `both-lanes-wide-*`; the document's smallest step is 11 px.
   - Proposal: raise edge labels to the micro-badge step, or show them only when a pathway is
     isolated or a step is selected.
   - Design document: none.
   - Effort: S. Value: low to medium (legibility of the signature surface).

9. **A missing age band renders as 0 %** (Overview ribbon ready, H4).
   - Observation: `features/overview/DemographicsCard.tsx:41` maps an absent band to `0`, so the
     segmented bar shows a 0 % share for data the API did not send.
   - Proposal: omit absent bands from the bar and list them as "not reported" under it.
   - Design document: none (rule exists).
   - Effort: S. Value: low (depends on API behaviour; verify with a cassette first).

## Design document changes (consolidated)

- Materials: empty sections take their content height; rows grow only with data.
- Materials: layering ladder (content < map overlays < covering view < drawers and dialogs).
- Copy: counts are shown only when there are items to count.
- Required states, Interactive: disabled controls say why.

## Not assessed

- Risk by group tab, facility inspector and pin popovers, worst-case section with scenarios,
  exposure chain picker with several facilities: need assessment data (no key in this run).
- Chat streaming, tool pills, confirmation card, error banner, usage meter: need a run.
- Clear-workspace confirm dialog and report download: need a workspace.
- Settings accordion contents and key save feedback: not opened to avoid touching stored keys.
- Monitoring session detail and timeline; monitoring empty and error states (Logfire returned
  data).
- Reduced-motion behaviour: not observable in screenshots; covered by the exposure chain's
  reduced-motion test only.
- Long text and many items (H9) on most surfaces.
